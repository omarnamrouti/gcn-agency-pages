What we do

You're on a roof, under a sink, or in a crawlspace, and the phone rings. If you don't answer, most callers hang up and call the next company. GCN Agency watches your business line. When a call goes unanswered, we send that caller a text within seconds that picks up the conversation: who you are, that you got their message, and when you can get back to them.

Missed calls get a reply

Every unanswered call gets a text, 24 hours a day, including nights and weekends.

Built for trade work

Plumbers, roofers, HVAC, electricians and fence companies in Louisville.

Simple reporting

Monthly summary of missed calls and text-backs, so you can see what the line is catching.

How it works

  1. We set up the service on your business line and test it with a real call before it goes live.
  2. A call goes unanswered. The caller gets a text from your business within seconds.
  3. The caller replies, and you (or your team) take it from there.
  4. You get a monthly report of missed calls and text-backs.
