GCN Agency Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

GCN Agency ("we", "us") provides missed-call text-back and customer messaging services for local home services businesses.

What we collect

When you call a business line we support or text that line, we receive your phone number and the content of your messages. We use this only to respond to your inquiry and coordinate service on behalf of that business.

How we use your data

We use your phone number and message content solely to deliver the text-back and reply service you requested. We do not sell, share, or disclose your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting out

You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from us. Reply HELP for assistance.
